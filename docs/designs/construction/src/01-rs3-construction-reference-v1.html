1. Scope, method and confidence

This document describes the live RS3 Construction skill and player-owned house (PoH) system as it stands after the 13 July 2026 rework and the patches through 21 September 2026. It exists to be the behavioural specification that 02 Rev-727 Technical Design ports from. It describes what the game does, not how Jagex implements it; implementation is inferred only where marked.

1.1 Evidence quality

Every wiki page was read through a fetcher that returns a model-written summary rather than raw page text, and several pages returned 404 or were disambiguation stubs. Numbers are therefore reported, not proven. The launch announcement, the Day-2 news and the September patch notes were all read through the same summariser, so none of them is a verbatim quote. Each statement carries a tag:

3. The central design change

The 2006–2025 house was a grid of purposeful rooms. A "Kitchen" room contained fixed hotspots (larder, stove, sink); you built one piece of furniture into each hotspot, and removing it forfeited the item. Training was the act of building and removing at hotspots.

The rework replaces all of that with a three-step loop:

  1. Craft an individual furniture object at a workbench, consuming materials and granting XP.
  2. Store it in furniture storage (a trunk separate from the bank).
  3. Place it on any tile in any room in build mode, move it, rotate it, swap it, or return it to storage without loss.

Rooms now carry no function; they are cosmetic shapes that define walkable space and where doorways go. VERIFIED (rework announcement and wiki 2026 update page agree). Furniture is no longer tied to room type, and the same piece can be placed many times.

4. Plot, rooms and limits

4.1 The plot

  • The house sits on a single square plot of 48 × 48 game squares (2,304 tiles). VERIFIED Two wiki pages agree.
  • A house has at most 25 rooms and 500 furniture objects at the top of the progression. REPORTED
  • There is an upper floor reachable by stairs; upper-floor doorways cannot be left empty (they must be wall, window or door). A known bug allowed an inaccessible upstairs room. REPORTED
  • Dungeons, basements and servants were not restored. REPORTED

4.4 Placing and connecting rooms

  • Two methods exist: attach a new room through an existing doorway, or position a whole room on the build grid with rotation. REPORTED
  • Rooms may be disconnected from each other; separated rooms must be at least 2 tiles apart. REPORTED
  • An unconnected doorway can be set to open doorway, filled wall or wall with window. REPORTED
  • Adding a room shows a confirmation screen (added Day 2). Moving a placed room was announced as in development on Day 2 and is listed as a feature on the wiki; the date it shipped is unknown. CONFLICT
  • Whether room positions snap to a 4-tile or 8-tile grid is not stated by any source. Hallways of width 4 and small squares of 4×4 suggest a 4-tile snap. INFERENCE This matters greatly to the 727 port (see document 2, section 5).

5.2 Placement rules

  • Any tile may take furniture; there are no hotspots. Duplicates of the same item are allowed. VERIFIED
  • Trees may not be placed indoors. Paintings and cobwebs go on walls. REPORTED
  • Ground decorations (rugs) allow other furniture to be stacked on top of them. REPORTED
  • Furniture not yet in storage appears faded in the build list: it can be previewed but not placed. REPORTED
  • The level check was removed from placement on Day 2 (you need the level to craft, not to place). REPORTED
  • Surface placement (an object on a table) and ceiling placement are not described by any fetched source; the existence of wall, floor, ground-decoration and surface categories is INFERENCE.

5.3 Restrictions in the house

  • Items dropped in build mode vanish when you leave. Logging out or switching world puts you outside the portal. Familiars cannot be summoned in build mode. Dueling is disabled. REPORTED

6. Furniture storage (the "trunk")

  • Holds up to 750 unique furniture items; separate from the bank. REPORTED
  • Furniture must be in storage to be placed; it cannot be placed straight from the inventory. REPORTED
  • Categories: Furnishings, Functional (altars, ranges, crafting stations, pets), Decor, Garden, Teleports, Lighting, Trophies. REPORTED
  • Accessible from a box outside the Rimmington portal. One page says deposit/withdraw is impossible inside the house while Jagex marketing lists a "Furniture Trunk" as house furniture. CONFLICT
  • Ex-members can withdraw members' furniture but not redeposit it. REPORTED
  • Other storage furniture: costume room, toy box, treasure chest, armour chest, cape rack, menagerie, aquarium. REPORTED

7.2 Recipes

  • Uses the Furniture Construction interface with materials in the inventory; hammer and saw come from the tool belt. REPORTED
  • Materials: planks (regular, oak, teak, mahogany, eternal), bars, cloth, nails, soft clay, magic stones, gold leaf, marble, limestone. REPORTED
  • XP per piece runs from small values up to 15,000 (Crystal Throne, level 95). Crystal saw +3, Prifddinas saw +5, chisel +1 and tea give level boosts. REPORTED
  • Recipes unlock by level, quest, exploration or shop purchase. REPORTED
  • The per-piece recipe and XP tables were not obtained.

9.3 Instancing

The house is an instanced pocket dimension reached through portals; houses are not tied to the portal used. VERIFIED The tutorial quest There's No Place Like Home… teaches placement and room building and is required before some other quests. REPORTED

10. Homesteads and visitors

  • A homestead is a shared neighbourhood instance of up to 5 players (owner plus four). Created via the housing wizard at the Rimmington portal; members invited by right-click within a time window; the group then names and finalises it. REPORTED
  • Members walk freely between each other's homes and use each other's facilities (ironmen cannot use others' workbenches or nexus). Public homesteads are searchable with unrestricted visitor numbers. Leaving returns the plot to a single home. REPORTED
  • Owners can kick a specific guest; guests visit by invitation or through a public homestead. REPORTED

11. Migration and what was not restored

  • Old rooms and walls were not preserved. Furniture that mapped onto a new object was converted into furniture storage; unconvertible items were logged for possible later restoration. VERIFIED
  • Not restored: player dungeons and their furniture, servants, portable workbenches. REPORTED
  • Housewarming gift furniture was given from 13 July to 13 August. REPORTED
