1. Scope, method and confidence

This document describes the live RS3 Construction skill and player-owned house (PoH) system after the 13 July 2026 rework and the patches through 21 September 2026. It is the behavioural specification that 02 Rev-727 Technical Design ports from. It describes what the game does, not how Jagex implements it.

3. The central design change

The 2006–2025 house was a grid of purposeful rooms with fixed hotspots: a Kitchen had a larder hotspot and a stove hotspot, you built one piece of furniture into each, and removing it forfeited the item.

The rework replaces that with a loop: craft furniture at a workbench, store it in furniture storage, place it on any tile in build mode, and move, rotate, swap or return it to storage with no loss. Rooms have no function; furniture is "decoupled from specific rooms"; duplicates are allowed. VERIFIED

The design goals state that houses should be "transitory": no direct bank access and no new skilling hubs, so players keep using the wider world. VERIFIED

4. Plot, rooms, floors and limits

4.1 The plot

  • One square plot of 48×48 game squares, "total, unchanging size". VERIFIED
  • Placement uses a build grid "based on the game square grid". VERIFIED
  • Player dungeons, basements and servants were not restored. REPORTED

4.3 Floors and stairs

  • A house has up to three floors. Ground-floor rooms from level 1, first-floor rooms from level 30, second-floor rooms from level 70. VERIFIED
  • Stairs are a room option: an "Add Stairs" checkbox in the room picker (level 30). The staircase is a spiral staircase in the centre of the room, styled by the house style. VERIFIED
  • If no room exists above, adding stairs creates one automatically and doubles the room's cost. Further rooms can then be added on that floor while standing on it. VERIFIED
  • Stairs cannot be added or removed except by deleting the room, but can be "edited" to add or remove the room above. This can strand upper floors if a connecting room is removed. VERIFIED
  • Balconies: an upper room may overhang the ground by being added from an upper-level room; beams are placed on the ground to support it. A room cannot be removed while another sits directly above it. On the second floor, rooms can only be built directly on top of a room of the same type. VERIFIED

4.5 Adding, moving and removing rooms

  • Two ways to add: click an existing doorway and choose "Add Room" (connects if there is space), or place the whole room freely on the grid and rotate it before clicking to commit. VERIFIED
  • Rooms need not be adjacent; separated rooms must be at least 2 tiles apart. VERIFIED Together with the tile-grid statement this indicates room positions are not locked to an 8-tile lattice. INFERENCE
  • To move a room: stand in it and choose "Edit Current Room" from the House Controls tab, or use "Edit Doorway". To remove: right-click a doorway inside it, "Edit doorway", "Remove room". VERIFIED

4.6 Doorways and floor styles

  • Each room has at least one doorway. Right-clicking one offers: add an adjacent room, remove the adjacent room, or (if unconnected) customise it. VERIFIED
  • An unconnected doorway can be a doorway, a wall with a window or a solid wall. Connected doorways must stay doorways. There is no way to add doors. Unconnected doorways on floors 2 and 3 cannot be empty. VERIFIED
  • Each room has an editable floor style (since 27 July). The wiki lists 14: wood plank (default), teak plank, mahogany plank, herringbone light, herringbone dark, paving stone, grey flagstone, red flagstone, dark stone slabs, terracotta paving, kitchen tile, temple tile, straw and mud. Prices 5,000–15,000 gp; several need a level, a quest or a shop plan. VERIFIED

5. Build mode

Build mode toggles seamlessly from the House Controls tab, even with visitors present. VERIFIED The build interface lives at the left of the screen and can be collapsed.

5.2 Placement rules

  • Furniture goes "freely on the game square tile system, with few restrictions". VERIFIED
  • Furniture may be placed even if you lack the Construction level to craft it. VERIFIED
  • Trees cannot go indoors. Paintings and cobwebs must go against a wall (interior or exterior). VERIFIED
  • Furniture generally cannot intersect other furniture (chairs cannot tuck under tables). Ground decorations such as rugs and grass are the exception: items can sit on them. VERIFIED
  • The indoor/outdoor filter is cosmetic; plants and grass may be placed indoors. VERIFIED
  • Furniture not in storage appears faded in the build list: it can be previewed, not placed. VERIFIED
  • Surface placement (an object on a table) and ceiling placement are not described. INFERENCE that they exist.

5.3 Instance rules

  • Items cannot be dropped normally in build mode; dropped items vanish when you leave. Fires and marker plants work in any mode but are removed on leaving. VERIFIED
  • Logging out or switching worlds places you outside the portal. Familiars cannot be summoned in build mode. Dueling is blocked. VERIFIED

6. Furniture storage

  • A dedicated, bank-like box outside the Rimmington house portal. It stores up to 750 unique furniture items; furniture must be stored there to be used in build mode and cannot be placed from the inventory. VERIFIED
  • Furniture cannot be deposited or withdrawn from inside the house, and is not part of the standard bank. This resolves the v1 conflict: marketing's "Furniture Trunk" is the Rimmington box. VERIFIED
  • Category tree: Furnishings (chairs, tables, fireplaces, bookcases, beds, storage, shelves, misc), Functional (altars & burners, cooking fires & ranges, storage, water source, aquarium gear, skilling items, lecterns, crafting stations, pets, misc), Teleports, Decor (rugs, clocks, armour stands, trophies, display items, wall decor), Lighting, Trophies, Garden, Misc (games). VERIFIED
  • Former members can withdraw members' furniture but not redeposit it. VERIFIED

9. Entry, styles and instancing

9.1 Portals and the spell

  • Nine house portals: Rimmington, Taverley, Pollnivneach, Rellekka, Brimhaven, Yanille, Prifddinas, Otot, Menaphos. Houses are not tied to a portal; any one may be used to enter, and the exit portal is selectable. Changing the default exit costs a fee and carries the same requirements as entering. VERIFIED
  • The House Teleport spell needs 40 Magic; a Teleport-to-house tablet needs no level. Settings choose whether teleports land inside the house or outside the portal. VERIFIED
  • Free-to-play worlds: enter only at Rimmington, exit at Rimmington or Taverley. VERIFIED
  • Per-portal level and fee figures (Rimmington 1 / 5,000 gp up to Prifddinas 75 and Plague's End) come from a summarised page. REPORTED
  • To start, complete the quest There's No Place Like Home… in Rimmington; it grants a plot with a simple structure and scrap furniture. VERIFIED

9.2 Styles

Five exterior styles set "the fabric of the house": Basic Wood (default), Basic Stone, Fancy Stone (members), Dark Stone (members, Love Story), Fremennik Wood (members). Applied through six estate agents or by right-click "Redecorate". The plot, scenery and sky are the same for every style. VERIFIED

9.3 Instancing

The house lives in an instanced pocket dimension; its scenery can vary with the plot it is assigned inside a homestead. VERIFIED

10. Homesteads

  • A shared space where the owner and up to four others build houses together. Members move between homes and use each other's facilities. Public homesteads allow unlimited visitors. VERIFIED
  • Created by the housing wizard outside the Rimmington portal ("Start a homestead"); invite nearby players during a limited window; name and finalise once there is more than one member. The name cannot be changed without recreating it. VERIFIED
  • Entry: right-click any portal, "Homestead centre" or "Visit homestead" (public list with search). Plots are numbered; swapping plots takes effect only after the homestead is unloaded by everyone. VERIFIED
  • Ironman players cannot use other players' workbenches or nexus. Cross-world behaviour and privacy are noted as unclear. VERIFIED

11. Migration and what was not restored

  • Old rooms and layouts were not preserved; furniture that mapped onto a new object was converted into furniture storage. VERIFIED
  • Not restored: player dungeons, servants, portable workbenches. REPORTED
